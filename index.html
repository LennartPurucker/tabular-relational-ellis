About the session

Foundation models have transformed language and vision, and are starting to transform tabular and relational data. Where does this lead? World models over tables, unified models across modalities, or something else? This session brings the tabular and relational communities together to look back on their progress and decide what comes next.

Scope
Any table-like data, including spreadsheets, multi-table data, and relational databases.
Who should come
Researchers from the tabular and relational communities, and junior researchers from other fields looking for open problems.
You leave with
Research directions, paper ideas, and new collaborators.

Call for in-person late-breaking posters

Coming to the ELLIS UnConference? Bring your newest work on AI for tabular and relational data.

We are looking to host late-breaking posters from people attending the UnConference in person. Late-breaking means work that has not been presented at any prior workshop or conference. This complements the UnConference’s general call for posters, which is for recently accepted papers.

Submit
A title and an abstract through the submission form.
Topics
Anything in the session’s scope, for example tabular foundation models, relational deep learning, spreadsheets and multi-table data, LLMs and agents for data science, multi-modal learning with tables, benchmarks, and efficiency.
Review
The organizers check each submission for scope and quality.
Format
In person only, in the UnConference poster session. There is no remote option. Accepted posters are not archived and not listed online.
Attend
The presenting author registers for the ELLIS UnConference, attends in Paris, and prints their own poster.

The submission form opens here on 21 November 2026.

Register for the UnConference

Only for people attending the ELLIS UnConference in person. Submitting a poster here does not register you for the UnConference; register separately through its registration page.
